Report No: QIR-2026-0788
Inspection Date: 2026-08-11
Inspector: L. Chen, Incoming Quality
Shipment Reference: SHP-15596
Supplier: MexicoSupply-017
Part: TEXT-0089

Quantity Received: 1159 units
Quantity Inspected (sample): 300 units
Quantity Rejected: 119 units (extrapolated from sample defect rate)

Findings:
Textile lot showed color variance beyond the approved Delta-E tolerance
(2.0) against the reference standard swatch; measured Delta-E ranged
from 2.3 to 3.8 across the sampled rolls. No dimensional or tensile
defects were found -- this is a purely cosmetic/color-match issue tied
to a dye-lot change the supplier did not flag on the packing list.

Root Cause: Supplier changed dye lot without notifying the buyer's
quality team, violating the change-notification clause in the supply
agreement.

Disposition: Rejected 119 units (rolls furthest outside tolerance).
Remaining rolls accepted for non-visible/internal-use parts only, per
engineering waiver EW-2026-091.

Corrective Action Requested: Supplier to reinstate dye-lot change
notification per contract Section 7.2 and submit a corrected Delta-E
report for the remaining inventory.

Follow-up: No prior color-related nonconformance on file for this
supplier; treated as an isolated process-control lapse, not a trend.
