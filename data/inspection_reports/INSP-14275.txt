SUPPLIER QUALITY INSPECTION REPORT

Report No: QIR-2026-0655
Inspection Date: 2026-06-23
Inspector: R. Alvarez, Incoming Quality
Shipment Reference: SHP-14275
Supplier: MexicoSupply-017
Part: MACH-0075

Quantity Received: 908 units
Quantity Inspected (sample): 908 units (100% inspection, flagged supplier)
Quantity Rejected: 113 units

Findings:
CMM dimensional check found the critical bore diameter out of tolerance
(target 12.700mm +/-0.02mm) on 113 units, measuring between 12.735mm and
12.751mm -- consistent with a worn boring tool that was not replaced on
schedule. Surface finish and hardness were within spec on all sampled
parts.

Root Cause: Tool wear beyond the supplier's preventive-maintenance
interval; supplier's tool-change log shows the boring tool was run
1,400 cycles past its documented 5,000-cycle replacement point.

Disposition: Reject 113 units. Remaining 795 units passed 100%
dimensional re-check and were accepted.

Corrective Action Requested: Supplier to enforce tool-change intervals
via the CNC controller's cycle-count lockout rather than a manual log,
and provide updated PM records.

Follow-up: This is the fourth machining-tolerance nonconformance from
MexicoSupply-017 for this part number in the past 12 months --
escalating to supplier corrective action board (SCAR) review.
