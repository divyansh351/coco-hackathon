SUPPLIER QUALITY INSPECTION REPORT

Report No: QIR-2025-0201
Inspection Date: 2025-04-22
Inspector: M. Singh, Incoming Quality
Shipment Reference: SHP-00390
Supplier: CanadaSupply-015
Part: MACH-0157

Quantity Received: 1086 units
Quantity Inspected (sample): 1086 units (100% inspection, per contract)
Quantity Rejected: 106 units

Findings:
Incoming inspection identified porosity defects in 106 units revealed by
dye-penetrant testing, concentrated near the casting's gate location.
Metallurgical review attributes this to insufficient degassing during
the casting process. No dimensional or material-composition issues were
found on any unit.

Root Cause: Casting process control -- inadequate degassing time before
pour, per supplier's own process audit shared during the investigation
call.

Disposition: Reject 106 units, scrap disposition (no rework possible
for porosity defects of this severity). Remaining 980 units accepted.

Corrective Action Requested: Supplier to extend degassing hold time per
their revised casting work instruction WI-CAST-09 Rev 4 and submit
first-article inspection results for the next production lot before
shipment.

Follow-up: First porosity-related nonconformance from this supplier in
the current contract period; no trend identified, but flagged for
monitoring on the next two shipments.
