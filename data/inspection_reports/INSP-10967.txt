SUPPLIER QUALITY INSPECTION REPORT

Report No: QIR-2026-0331
Inspection Date: 2026-03-16
Inspector: L. Chen, Incoming Quality
Shipment Reference: SHP-10967
Supplier: MexicoSupply-017
Part: MACH-0145

Quantity Received: 629 units
Quantity Inspected (sample): 629 units (100% inspection, flagged supplier)
Quantity Rejected: 104 units

Findings:
Torque test on the threaded insert failed on 104 units -- inserts pulled
out below the 45 Nm minimum holding torque, with observed values as low
as 28 Nm. Thread pitch measured within spec, so the failure is
adhesive-related rather than a machining defect.

Root Cause: Supplier's threadlocker adhesive cure time was reduced during
a recent line-speed increase; insufficient cure time before torque test
weakens the bond.

Disposition: Reject 104 units. Remaining 525 units held 48 hours for
full adhesive cure, then re-tested and passed.

Corrective Action Requested: Supplier to revert cure-time to the
validated process parameter or requalify the faster cycle with a full
DOE (design of experiments) study before resuming the higher line speed.

Follow-up: New nonconformance mode for this supplier -- flagged as a
process-change control gap, escalate to supplier's plant manager.
