SUPPLIER QUALITY INSPECTION REPORT

Report No: QIR-2026-0298
Inspection Date: 2026-03-05
Inspector: R. Alvarez, Incoming Quality
Shipment Reference: SHP-10433
Supplier: MexicoSupply-017
Part: PACK-0150

Quantity Received: 1091 units
Quantity Inspected (sample): 300 units
Quantity Rejected: 99 units (extrapolated from sample defect rate)

Findings:
Carton crush-strength test failed on 27 of 300 sampled units (9%); the
corrugate board's edge crush test (ECT) measured 28-30 lb/in against a
32 lb/in minimum. Visual inspection also found inconsistent glue-flap
adhesion on a subset of the same failing units.

Root Cause: Supplier substituted a lower-grade corrugate board without
a documented material change request, likely as a cost-reduction measure
by their sub-tier board supplier.

Disposition: Reject 99 units. Remaining shipment accepted for
non-stacked, single-layer storage only, per warehouse ops waiver.

Corrective Action Requested: Supplier to revert to the approved
corrugate board grade and submit a Material Change Request (MCR) for
any future substitution, per the supplier quality manual Section 4.

Follow-up: First material-substitution nonconformance on file for this
supplier; recommend an unannounced sub-tier supplier audit.
