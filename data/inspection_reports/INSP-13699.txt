SUPPLIER QUALITY INSPECTION REPORT

Report No: QIR-2026-0611
Inspection Date: 2026-06-03
Inspector: R. Alvarez, Incoming Quality
Shipment Reference: SHP-13699
Supplier: MexicoSupply-017
Part: RAW -0044

Quantity Received: 856 units
Quantity Inspected (sample): 856 units (100% inspection, per SCAR history)
Quantity Rejected: 141 units

Findings:
Visual inspection of the raw material coils found surface oxidation across
multiple pallets, consistent with prolonged exposure to humidity during
ocean transit. Approximately 16% of the lot showed rust discoloration
exceeding the acceptable cosmetic tolerance in spec MS-114 Rev C. A
sub-sample of 20 units was cut and tested; 3 units also failed thickness
tolerance (+/- 0.05mm), suggesting a secondary rolling-process deviation
unrelated to the moisture issue.

Root Cause: Packaging/moisture barrier failure during transit (primary);
supplier rolling-mill calibration drift (secondary, contributing).

Disposition: Reject and return 141 units. Remaining 715 units accepted
with concession, pending 8D corrective action report from supplier.

Corrective Action Requested: Supplier to add desiccant packs and vapor
barrier film to all future coil shipments; recalibrate rolling mill
gauge sensors and provide calibration certificate.

Follow-up: This is the third moisture-related rejection from
MexicoSupply-017 in the last two quarters. Recommend supplier
performance review and consideration of tier downgrade if unresolved
by next shipment.
