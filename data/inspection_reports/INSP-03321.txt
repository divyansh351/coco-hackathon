SUPPLIER QUALITY INSPECTION REPORT

Report No: QIR-2025-0369
Inspection Date: 2025-08-04
Inspector: T. Okafor, Incoming Quality
Shipment Reference: SHP-03321
Supplier: MexicoSupply-017
Part: MACH-0075

Quantity Received: 1111 units
Quantity Inspected (sample): 400 units
Quantity Rejected: 105 units (extrapolated from sample defect rate)

Findings:
Sampled units showed inconsistent bore diameter, with 38 of 400 sampled
parts (9.5%) outside the +/-0.02mm tolerance band. Pattern matches a
known tool-wear signature previously seen on this part number from this
same supplier.

Root Cause: Repeat of prior tool-wear nonconformance (see QIR-2026-0655);
supplier's corrective action from the earlier event had not yet taken
effect at the time this shipment was produced.

Disposition: Reject 105 units. Remaining shipment released after
100% re-check of the affected dimension.

Corrective Action Requested: Confirm effective date of the CNC
cycle-count lockout corrective action and require supplier to certify,
in writing, that all shipments after that date were produced under the
new control.

Follow-up: Pattern confirms a systemic tool-maintenance issue for this
part number at this supplier, not an isolated event.
