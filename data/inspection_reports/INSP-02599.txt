SUPPLIER QUALITY INSPECTION REPORT

Report No: QIR-2025-0602
Inspection Date: 2025-07-25
Inspector: T. Okafor, Incoming Quality
Shipment Reference: SHP-02599
Supplier: IndiaSupply-010
Part: ELEC-0054

Quantity Received: 1212 units
Quantity Inspected (sample): 1212 units (100% inspection, per contract)
Quantity Rejected: 91 units

Findings:
Functional test bench failed 91 units on the power-on self-test, with
intermittent failures traced to a cold solder joint on the main PCB's
voltage regulator pad. X-ray inspection of a 10-unit subset confirmed
insufficient solder reflow on the affected pad across all 10 units
checked.

Root Cause: Reflow oven temperature profile drift on the supplier's
SMT line; oven zone 3 was running 8-10C below the qualified profile at
the time this lot was produced, per supplier's own SPC data shared
after the investigation request.

Disposition: Reject 91 units, no rework (board-level rework not
authorized for safety-critical boards). Remaining 1121 units passed
100% functional test and were accepted.

Corrective Action Requested: Supplier to recalibrate the reflow oven's
zone 3 thermocouple, verify with a full thermal profile run, and provide
first-article X-ray results for the next three lots.

Follow-up: No prior reflow-related nonconformance from this supplier;
isolated equipment-drift event, not a trend -- standard monitoring only.
