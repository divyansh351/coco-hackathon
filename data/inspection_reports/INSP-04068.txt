SUPPLIER QUALITY INSPECTION REPORT

Report No: QIR-2025-0442
Inspection Date: 2025-08-31
Inspector: T. Okafor, Incoming Quality
Shipment Reference: SHP-04068
Supplier: MexicoSupply-017
Part: PACK-0171

Quantity Received: 776 units
Quantity Inspected (sample): 200 units
Quantity Rejected: 135 units (extrapolated from sample defect rate)

Findings:
Sample inspection of packaging components revealed inconsistent heat-seal
strength on the film liners; 34 of 200 sampled units failed the peel-force
test (below 4.5 N minimum per spec PKG-22). Seal defects were concentrated
in units from the second half of the shipment, suggesting a mid-run
equipment issue on the supplier's sealing line rather than a
material-batch problem.

Root Cause: Sealing bar temperature drift during the production run
(supplier process control issue), not a raw material defect.

Disposition: Full shipment placed on hold pending 100% re-inspection.
135 units confirmed defective and rejected; balance released to
production after re-test.

Corrective Action Requested: Supplier to implement in-line temperature
monitoring with automatic reject on the sealing station and submit
process capability data (Cpk) for the next three production runs.

Follow-up: Second sealing-related defect from this supplier this year.
